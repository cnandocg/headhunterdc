“Let the rise of the damned begin!!!”

Headhunter D.C. returns with the unholy fury of “Rise of the Damned...”, their sixth studio album!

In a cry of resistance and unwavering passion, Headhunter D.C., a pillar of Brazilian Death Metal, releases their highly anticipated new album, “Rise of the Damned...”, their sixth album of original material in their long career. Released on the very appropriate date of 06/06/2026, this work marks the band’s return to full-length studio albums after a 14-year hiatus since the acclaimed “...In Unholy Mourning...” (2012). The label responsible is Mutilation Records, continuing a long partnership that has lasted for over 25 years.

“A lot of time has passed and many things, good and bad, have happened between 2012 and 2026, but obviously it was the hardships that led us to yet another record delay in releasing a new album of original songs. The ‘5-year curse’ of yesteryear has given way to 14 long years!”, declares the leader and vocalist Sérgio “Nekrobaloff666” Borges, promising that this will be the last delay of such magnitude in his career. “Rise of the Damned...” is a declaration that the band’s long and arduous journey, always marked by great challenges, has only strengthened its essence.

The current Headhunter D.C. lineup features the vital force of Sérgio “Nekrobaloff666” Borges on vocals, Danilo “D. Morbidus” Coimbra on guitars, Héracles “Demigod” Cardoso on bass and the recently summoned Juan “Death Ax” Machado on drums and Michael “Hellriff” Gonçalves on second guitar. This lineup represents the highest degree of resistance in a period of great turbulence, delivering a powerful and massive material that faithfully synthesizes the ideology of rebellion and the viscerally deathmetallic sound that defines Headhunter Death Cult.

The period after 2012 was not just one of waiting, but of intense activity and trials. The band celebrated historic concerts, including a European tour through 9 countries in 2013, the highest number of performances per annum since its founding in 1987. Consistent releases, such as participation in 5 compilations (including 3 tribute ones), 2 live albums and a cover compilation of their own, kept the flame burning during the aforementioned period.

However, from 2016 onwards, the band faced personal and structural setbacks. The loss of Sérgio’s sister in May 2016, to whom this album is dedicated, and the departure of founder Paulo Lisboa in 2017, after almost 30 years of partnership, were difficult moments. “These were two major and painful blows in different spheres,” says Borges, who has the special participation of Paulo Lisboa on this new album, among other no less important guests. The succession of lineup changes, culminating in the departure of two members in mid-2025, once again shook the structures and delayed the process, but solidified the resilience of the current quintet, which has finally achieved the long-awaited reformulation of the Cult to hit the road again and promote the album.

“Rise of the Damned...” transcends music; it’s a kind of “cry of the excluded”, an ascent over everything that has tried to stifle the band in recent times. It’s the “black revenge” against the falsehood and hypocrisy of the world, an explosion of passion and resistance that marks not only the end of a turbulent cycle, but the beginning of a new era for Headhunter D.C. “It’s pure, brutal, unsettling, and haunting old-school Death Metal emerging from the depths of our dark minds, hearts, and souls”, describes Borges, emphasizing the band’s commitment to always surpassing themselves and defending their legacy.

This album is a promise fulfilled to all deathcultists who (im)patiently awaited another offering from the Brazilian Death Cult. A testament to almost 40 years of unwavering dedication to Underground Death Metal.

About Headhunter D.C.

Founded in 1987, Headhunter D.C. is one of the pillars of Brazilian and South American Extreme Metal, pioneers of Death Metal in the country and the oldest band of the genre still active in Brazil, revered for its aggressive sound, unholy atmospheres and impactful lyrics, as well as its always brutal and memorable live performances. With a discography that has influenced generations, the band maintains a global fanbase, consolidating its legacy in the Death Metal scene.
